# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve InstallSentry.

1. Open an issue for bugs, compatibility reports, or proposed features.
2. Keep changes focused and explain their user and privacy impact.
3. Do not add network access, tracking, or new sensitive permissions without a clear explanation and maintainer review.
4. Test on the Android versions and device types affected by your change.
5. Ensure `gradle assembleDebug` succeeds and update documentation when behavior changes.
6. Never include private keys, credentials, or personal device information in commits.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under the project's MIT license.
